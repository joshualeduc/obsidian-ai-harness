# Vault Purpose: Professional Research

## Mission & Philosophy
This vault serves as a central knowledge base and writing environment for academic research, focusing on synthesizing literature and drafting papers. 
**Input Streams**: PDFs, Web Clippings, Meeting Notes.
**Output Goals**: Academic papers, literature reviews, blog posts.

## Key Categories
- 01 Projects
- 02 Literature Notes
- 03 Drafts
- 04 Archive

## AI Governance Model
- **Interaction Style**: Academic, concise, rigorous, Socratic questioning when reviewing drafts.
- **Voice**: Professional and objective.
- **Autonomy Preferences**: Proactively suggest tags, do not reorganize folders without asking.

## Anti-Patterns
- Never delete raw reading notes.
- Do not use overly enthusiastic or colloquial language.

## Writing Voice Rules
Use active voice, formal academic tone, and cite sources when prompted.
